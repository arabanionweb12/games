Souq & Football Day — Arabanion
================================
Upload this folder (just 2 files) into your games repo. All voices and pictures are built inside index.html.
Link: https://games.arabanion.com/teta-souq/

Teacher PIN: 2026  (🔑 button: stop codes, jump to any level, audio check of every clip, reset)

STOP codes (secret colours):
  Sami:  num = red, blue, yellow | want = green, red, white | dir = yellow, black, blue | final = blue, green, red
  Dean & Lina: time = white, yellow, green | market = black, red, blue | dir = yellow, black, blue | final = blue, green, red

Voices (ElevenLabs eleven_v4, Levantine/Jordanian):
  Teacher = Farah (Jordanian), Teta = Haneen, Zaatar = Fadi (also speaks the English instructions),
  Abu Khalil = Faris, Ghoul = Rami. Letter names were generated from exact IPA (ba, ta, tha, jeem, Ha, kha).
Magic Pencil: the mouse turns into a pencil; the child draws over the letter from the green dot, then taps the dots.
A pointing hand + English voice tells the child what to do the first time in every level (twice for Sami).
Recording works on the device that runs the game. On Zoom screen-share, use the green "✅ Mohamed: well said!" button.
